Accessibility

PMP Healthcare Corp. wants every person in Ontario to be able to find us, read this site and book care. This site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA and is checked with automated testing and by keyboard.

Other ways to book

You do not need the online form. Email clinic@pmphealthcare.ca with your name, a way to reach you and what you need. We reply within one business day. If you use a screen reader, screen magnifier, voice control or keyboard only, tell us and we will arrange the visit in the way that works for you, for example phone instead of video.

Other formats

Ask for any information on this site, or any visit document, in large print, plain text or another accessible format. Email us and we will provide it as soon as we can.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use, email clinic@pmphealthcare.ca with the page and what happened. We read every message and fix problems as quickly as we can.

Language

Services are offered in English. Les services sont offerts en anglais.
