Questions

Before you book.

Is this covered by OHIP?

No. Independent Nurse Practitioner care in Ontario is private-pay. Most workplace health plans reimburse NP services under paramedical or "nurse practitioner" benefits. Every visit comes with a receipt showing the NP's registration number. Send us your plan name and we'll tell you what we know.

Do I need a family doctor or a referral?

No. Book directly. If you have a family doctor, we send them a summary with your permission so nothing falls between the cracks.

What can't you treat virtually?

Emergencies: chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe shortness of breath, serious injury. Anything that needs a hands-on exam the same day. Controlled substances on a first visit. We'll tell you straight away if your concern needs to be seen in person.

Can a Nurse Practitioner prescribe?

Yes. NPs in Ontario prescribe medications, including controlled substances where the NP has completed the required education, order labs and imaging, and refer to specialists. A few things remain outside NP scope and we'll tell you when a physician is needed.

Can I book for a parent in a retirement home or nursing home?

Yes. Book a virtual visit or in-person assessment on their behalf. If they can consent, we confirm it with them at the start. If you're the substitute decision-maker, tell us. For on-site visits we first check the facility's own policies on outside clinicians and contact your parent's current primary care provider, so the home, their doctor and we are all working from the same plan.

I need a referral to a specific specialist. Can you do that?

Yes. Tell us what you need and why. We review your history, assess you, and refer to the appropriate specialist with the supporting notes and requisitions. Specialists set their own wait times and some require specific work-up first; we'll tell you what to expect.

How does a facility visiting agreement work?

We agree a weekly block, a resident list, and how to reach us between visits. We document in your chart, write orders within NP scope, and work with your Medical Director and attending physicians. Invoiced monthly. No minimum term for the first 90 days.

What do I need for a video visit?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera, a reasonable internet connection, and a private spot. We send a link by email. If video fails, we switch to phone.

Where are you?

Sarnia, Ontario. Virtual visits province-wide. In-person visits in Sarnia–Lambton, with travel to neighbouring areas by arrangement.
